About Me

My name is Douglas Vanderland, and I am a recent Computer Science graduate from Flinders University. I have a strong passion for everything technology-related, and I am constantly seeking new opportunities to learn and grow every day. I have a range of experience in technical and non-technical areas, including programming, web development, networking and project management.


I grew up in Adelaide for the first 10 years of my life, before moving to Karratha, Western Australia, where I spent the next 10 years of my life. During this time I had many different experiences, ivolving both school and my new work life post graduation. While living in Karratha, I attended the St. Luke's Anglican School before moving to Scotch College in Adelaide for my final four years of high school. This was done through the boarding progmram, which allowed me to live on campus and focus on my studies. After graduating from high school, I pursued work right away and got my first jobs as kitchen staff at Hungry Jacks and then a trolley pusher at Coles. I then moved to a more hands-on role as a baggage handler at Menzies Aviation loading the helicopters that flew to the local offshore oil rigs. I decided I needed to pursue further education, so I found an apprenticeship as a first class machinist at a local engineering company called KSIS. I worked there for one year before deciding it was not for me, and I wanted to pursue a career in technology instead. This is when I decided to move back to Adelaide and study Computer Science at Flinders University. While studying, I also worked as a meter reader for Service Stream, which allowed me to suppor myself financially while studying. I graduated from Flinders University with a Bachelor of Computer Science in December of 2026, and I am now looking for my dream job in the technology industry.
